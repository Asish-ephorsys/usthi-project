Every meaningful change begins with a vision—and grows into a revolution when people come together to support it. Education

“Education does not transform the World. Education changes people. People change the World.”
– Paulo Freire

We provide free and quality education to underprivileged children, helping them gain knowledge, confidence, and equal opportunities for a better future.

Our education program supports children with books, school supplies, trained teachers, digital learning tools, and personal mentorship. We focus on holistic development by nurturing creativity, discipline, and life skills so every child can grow, learn, and become self-reliant and empowered.

Environment

Nature may not speak, but its signs are clear when we choose to listen. By caring for our environment, we take responsibility to protect ecosystems, preserve resources, and safeguard the planet for future generations. Through tree plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns, waste management awareness, and sustainable practices, we work to build greener and healthier communities. Every small action counts, and collective effort can create lasting environmental change.

– Care for Nature, Care for Life

Health

“Health is not just the absence of illness, but a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being.”
– World Health Organization

We work to improve community health by organizing free medical camps, health awareness programs, and preventive care initiatives for underprivileged populations.

Our focus includes maternal and child health, nutrition awareness, hygiene education, and access to basic healthcare services, ensuring healthier and more resilient communities.

Who We Are

USTHI FOUNDATION INDIA (Cares for Blossoms in the Dust)

USTHI Foundation India established in the year 1977 is a non-profit social welfare program devoted to improving humanitarian aid and support for men, women and children belonging to underprivileged sections of society.

The organization started its work in a remote village of South 24 Parganas with a mission to create a sustainable and equitable society where everyone has an opportunity to thrive with meaningful contribution.

Today it operates programs on education, community health, vocational training and disaster relief across Odisha and West Bengal.
